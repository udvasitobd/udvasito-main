## 2. The problem

Evidence carried over from the pitch deck (sources as cited there):

- Bangladesh needs roughly **9.5–10 lakh bags of blood a year**; about **7.5 lakh** were collected and transfused in 2021 — a shortfall of **2–2.5 lakh bags a year** (The Business Standard, 13 Jun 2022, citing the DGHS Safe Blood Transfusion Programme).
- **68 %** of supply comes from relatives and friends: two in three bags still depend on a family finding a donor in a hurry.
- In a 2022 study of 7,289 donors in Chattogram, AB was **8.6 %** and Rh-negative only **~4.5 %** (about 1 in 22) — rare groups are the hardest to find by calling around (Dey et al., Chattagram Maa-O-Shishu Hosp. Med. Coll. J. 23(1), 2024).

What a family does today when a patient needs blood:

1. Posts in several Facebook groups and WhatsApp chats; the same request is copied, mutated and goes stale.
2. Calls numbers from old lists one by one — many are wrong, ineligible (donated recently) or asleep.
3. Volunteers duplicate each other's work; nobody knows which requests are already solved.
4. There is no dependable view of who is available, by blood group and area, right now.

**Dengue makes it worse.** In 2026, DGHS had recorded **128,237 dengue cases and 400 deaths by 27 October** (reported by News On AIR), and severe dengue cases often need **platelet** donors — a request families struggle to fill by calling around. UDVASITO treats platelet requests as a first-class, featured flow (user story US-R14).

**The real gap is coordination, not a lack of willing donors.**

### Lessons from v0 (why this is a rebuild, not a port)

| v0 did this | Problem | v1 must instead |
|---|---|---|
| Published a searchable list of donors with phone/WhatsApp | Scraping, harassment, spam calls, privacy-law exposure; families call people who are unavailable | Match requests to donors and share contact **only after the donor agrees** |
| Donors asked an admin to approve every availability change | Manual bottleneck; data goes stale | Donors update their own status instantly; cooldown is computed automatically |
| Client app posted every registration's full PII to an admin-pasted Google Apps Script URL | Data exfiltration risk | Exports are server-side, admin-only, logged |
| "AI integration — planned" | Nothing built | AI built into the core flows, with guardrails |
| Stats counter updated "on the side", errors swallowed | Numbers drift | Numbers derived from the source of truth by the backend |
| Hidden 5-tap seed button in production | Anyone could poison the data | No debug code paths in production builds |

**Worth keeping from v0:** the name and logo (blood drop + cross), the taglines *"Find blood donors when time matters"* and *"Join, Donate, Save lives"*, the red brand family (`#E63946` primary, `#C1121F` deep red, `#1D3557` navy secondary — final palette set in Step 6), the simple bottom-tab mental model (Home / Find / Join / More), multi-step registration, the live blood-pool chart and 30-day trend, volunteer registration, CSV import/export.

## 3. Vision statement — CHOSEN: Version A + Version C

> For families in Bangladesh who suddenly need blood for a patient, **UDVASITO turns a panicked search through scattered Facebook posts, WhatsApp forwards and old phone lists into one clear, verified request that reaches the right donors in minutes.** Anyone can type the request, say it in Bangla, paste a post they found or photograph the hospital slip; **AI pulls out the blood group, hospital and deadline and spots duplicates of the same request**, and the person confirms before anything is sent. **Transparent rules — not AI — then match the request to compatible, eligible, nearby donors and notify them**, and a donor's phone number is **shared only after that donor taps "I can help"**, so donors stay safe and families only call people who are actually available. UDVASITO starts in **Chattogram — universities first, then colleges, then the general public** — on a data model ready for **every district of Bangladesh**. It is **dengue-ready** (families can ask specifically for platelet donors), free to use, fully bilingual (English by default, Bangla one tap away), works on a cheap Android phone or any browser on a weak connection, and **runs itself**: requests expire, reminders go out, numbers update and suspicious activity is flagged without anyone maintaining a spreadsheet.

**One-line pitch:** One request, the right donor, minutes not hours — without exposing anyone's phone number.

**Bangla tagline (proposed — team to confirm wording):** রক্তের খোঁজে আর ছোটাছুটি নয় — এক অনুরোধে সঠিক দাতা, মিনিটের মধ্যে।

### What makes it different

1. **Consent-gated contact.** No public phone list. A donor's number is revealed to one requester, for one request, only when the donor accepts. Phone verification tells us *who* someone is; it does not make sharing donors' numbers safe, so consent is still required.
2. **AI intake for real-world mess (from Version C).** Typed Bangla/Banglish, a Bangla voice note, a pasted Facebook/WhatsApp post or a slip photo becomes a structured request that the person checks and confirms.
3. **One request, not fifty copies (from Version C).** Rules detect likely duplicates (same blood group + hospital + time window + contact), AI suggests "looks like the same request", and volunteers merge them — so the network works one shared board instead of scattered threads.
4. **Rules you can explain to a judge.** Blood-group compatibility, the donation-interval cooldown and distance are deterministic code, visible and testable — AI never decides who gets a call.
5. **A human within 30 seconds.** On-duty volunteers see every request in their area the moment it is posted; if no donor has accepted within 30 seconds, the family gets an on-duty volunteer's number — someone who chose to help (G-02).
6. **Self-running operations.** Expiry, reminders, cooldowns, duplicate detection and stats are automated jobs; admins handle exceptions, not routine.
7. **Dengue-ready.** A one-tap "Dengue patient → platelets" request reaches only donors who opted in to platelet donation.
8. **Honest impact.** We count donations, not just matches — every request is tracked through *posted → donors notified → donor accepted → contact shared → donation confirmed*.

Judges see the **live, functional platform — not a demo** (principle 10, D12). ## 6. AI feasibility check — can free tiers do Version C? (researched 2026-11-23)

**Short answer: yes for capacity, with design constraints from the free-tier terms.**

### Capacity
- As read in Google AI Studio on 2026-10-21 (reported by imisofts.com and aifreeapi.com): free tier gives **Gemini 3.5 / 3.1 Flash-Lite ≈ 500 requests/day at 15 RPM**, **Gemini 3.8 Flash ≈ 20 requests/day at 5 RPM**, and **0** for Pro models. Older guides quoting 1,500/day are out of date. Limits are per project, change without notice, and reset at midnight Pacific time.
- Pilot estimate: ≤ 50 requests/day × 1–2 AI calls + assistant questions ≈ well under 500/day. **Flash-Lite is the workhorse; Flash is reserved for rare hard cases.**
- When quota runs out, **every flow still works manually** (the request form is always there). A second free provider is evaluated in Step 5 as fallback.

### Terms that shape the design (Gemini API Additional Terms, unpaid services)
- On the unpaid quota, Google uses submitted content and responses to improve its products, human reviewers may read inputs and outputs, and the terms say **not to submit sensitive, confidential or personal information**.
- The terms forbid using the service **in clinical practice or to give medical advice**.
- The version reviewed also says the service must not be part of an app **directed at or likely used by people under 18**. (Current version re-checked in Step 5.)

### What each AI feature does under these rules

| AI feature | Model / method | Personal-data handling | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Typed request (Bangla / Banglish / English) → structured fields | Flash-Lite, 1 call | Code removes phone numbers, text after name labels ("রোগী / নাম / Patient / Name") and known diagnosis words **before** the AI call; the prompt asks only for blood group, units, hospital, area, deadline, urgency; users are told about AI processing on the consent screen (G-12) | ✅ Free |
| Bangla voice note → request | **Phone/browser built-in speech recognition** (Android speech recogniser / browser Web Speech API — often Google's speech service; free) → then same as typed | UDVASITO never receives audio; audio never goes to the AI provider; disclosed on the consent screen | ✅ Free |
| Pasted Facebook / WhatsApp post → request (Version C) | Flash-Lite, 1 call | Same redaction as typed text; contact number taken by regex, not AI | ✅ Free |
| Duplicate detection (Version C) | **Rules first** (blood group + hospital + time window + hashed contact); Flash-Lite only for a "probably the same?" hint | Only redacted fields compared | ✅ Free |
| Hospital requisition-slip photo → verified badge | Vision model needed | Slip shows patient name and clinical details → **must not go to the free tier** | ⚠ **Manual only** (budget ৳0): a volunteer/moderator checks the photo and grants the "verified" badge; the photo is private and auto-deleted when the request closes. The request is **never blocked** waiting for this check |
| "Can I donate?" assistant (Bangla / English) | Flash-Lite over a **fixed, team-reviewed FAQ**; common answers cached | Users told not to type personal details; the eligibility verdict comes from rules, not AI; always "ask a doctor" for anything clinical | ✅ Free |
| Shareable appeal text / card | Flash-Lite, optional | Uses only blood group, hospital, area, deadline | ✅ Free |
| Admin weekly summary + anomaly hints | Flash-Lite, ~1 call/week | Aggregated numbers only | ✅ Free |

**Consequence for the product:** UDVASITO accounts are **18+** (age confirmed at sign-up). This also matches the minimum donor age, but affects the college wave (§7).

## 7. Rollout plan (geography and audience)

- **Data model is nationwide from day one:** Division → District → Upazila/Thana, plus a free-text area and optional approximate location. Hospitals table seeded for Chattogram first.
- **Wave 1 — Chattogram universities:** University of Chittagong first, then other campuses (CUET, Chittagong Medical College, CVASU, private universities from the v0 list). Campus drives run by the team.
- **Wave 2 — Chattogram colleges:** **18+ students only** (degree / honours / older students). Most HSC students are under 18 and can neither donate nor use an app built on the Gemini free tier.
- **Wave 3 — general public in Chattogram,** then other districts, each opened when local volunteers and moderation capacity exist (the deck's guardrail: grow only as fast as trust allows).

## 10. Guiding principles (proposed — approve or edit)

0. **Speed first on the emergency path.** When blood is needed there is very little time. Nothing between "I need blood" and "donors notified" waits on AI, an admin approval, a slip check or an optional field. AI fills fields while the person types; the manual form always works; a returning user posts a request in ≤ 60 s with no extra verification.
1. **Privacy by default.** No public contact details. Collect only what matching needs. Donors can pause, hide or delete their account and data at any time. Sensitive images are private and auto-deleted.
2. **Rules decide, AI assists.** Compatibility, eligibility and ranking are deterministic and testable. AI parses, explains, drafts and flags; a person confirms. If AI is down or over quota, every flow still works manually.
3. **Minimum personal data to AI.** Code removes phone numbers, labelled names, addresses and diagnosis words before any AI call; voice is converted to text by the phone's or browser's own speech service (UDVASITO never receives audio); users are told about AI processing on the consent screen. Consent does not override the AI provider's terms, so the code redaction always runs (G-12).
4. **Not a medical service.** UDVASITO connects people. Screening, cross-matching and the decision to transfuse stay with hospitals and blood banks; AI never gives medical advice. Stated in onboarding and on every request.
5. **18+ only.** Date of birth is required at sign-up; under-18s cannot create accounts (minimum donor age and AI-provider terms — G-13).
6. **Fully bilingual, low-bandwidth-first, accessible.** English by default; the app opens in Bangla on Bangla-language phones and browsers, shared links open in the language they were shared in, and a one-tap Bangla/English switch is on every screen (G-17); every word available in both languages. Works on 2 GB-RAM Android phones and slow connections; WCAG 2.2 AA; large touch targets; plain language.
7. **Automate the routine, escalate the exception.** No manual approval for routine events. Admins see a queue of exceptions only (flags, reports, stuck requests, slip checks).
8. **৳0 to run.** Free tiers everywhere, no credit card on any account. A feature that cannot be free is done manually or deferred.
9. **Honest metrics.** Report every funnel stage; never present a match as a donation; publish community-funding totals.
10. **Real, working software — never a mock-up.** Judges and users see the live platform. Production holds only real data; every visible button works; nothing is faked, staged or shown as "coming soon". A feature that isn't finished is hidden until it fully works. The practice area exists only so that trying the platform never alerts real donors — it runs the same code with real phones and real numbers.
