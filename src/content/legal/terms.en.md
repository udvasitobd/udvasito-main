# Terms of use

These terms are the rules for using UDVASITO. By using the app or the website you agree to them and to our [Privacy notice](/privacy).

## What UDVASITO is — and is not

- UDVASITO is a **free** service run by **UDVASITO — a student initiative, University of Chittagong**. It helps people who need blood find willing donors.
- UDVASITO is **not a blood bank, a hospital or a medical service**. We do not collect, test, store or give blood, and we do not give medical advice.
- **We cannot promise that a donor will be found** or that a donor will come. In an emergency, also contact the hospital's blood bank directly.
- Whether someone may donate is decided by the blood bank or hospital that takes the blood, after its own checks.

## Who may use it

- You must be **18 or older**.
- Donors must be within the donor age range and meet the health conditions shown in the app. The app alerts you only when you are allowed to donate again.
- Use one account, in your own name, with **your own** mobile number.

## Your responsibilities

- Give true information: your name, date of birth, phone number, blood group and last donation date.
- Post a request only for a **real** patient who needs blood. Ask the patient or their family before you enter the patient's name, ward or bed, or a slip photo.
- Close your request as soon as the blood is arranged or no longer needed, so donors are not called for nothing.
- If you said "I can help", go — or tell the requester as early as possible that you cannot.
- Use phone numbers you receive **only** for that request. Do not save them for other purposes, share them or contact people afterwards without their agreement.
- Check every "AI suggested" field before you post. You are responsible for what you post.
- Use the **practice area** only to practise. Practice requests never reach real donors.

## Never pay or ask for money

**UDVASITO never asks for money. Never send money to anyone for blood.** Buying or selling blood is not allowed on UDVASITO. Report anyone who asks for money, and meet at the hospital or its blood bank.

## Not allowed

- fake, joke or duplicate requests;
- asking for or offering money, gifts or favours for blood;
- harassing, threatening or deceiving anyone, or contacting people for anything other than the request;
- using someone else's number or account, or creating accounts in bulk;
- collecting other people's data, copying the service, or trying to break, overload or get around its limits and protections.

## Reports, blocks and account action

You can report a request or a person and block someone. To keep people safe, our volunteers and admins may hide requests, freeze, suspend or ban accounts, and limit features — for example, an unverified phone number allows only one open request. If action is taken on your account, the app tells you, and you can write to us if you think it was a mistake.

## AI suggestions

When you type, paste or speak a request, an AI service may suggest how to fill in the form. Suggestions can be wrong. Only you decide what is posted. AI never decides who is contacted — fixed, tested rules do.

## The service

- UDVASITO is free and run by volunteers on free services. It may sometimes be slow, unavailable or have mistakes. We may change, pause or end features.
- Alerts depend on your phone, its settings and the network. Keep notifications on and follow the alert guide in the app.
