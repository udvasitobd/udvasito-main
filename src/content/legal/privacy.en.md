# Privacy notice

This notice explains what UDVASITO collects, why, who can see it, how long we keep it and what you can do about it. We wrote it in plain words. If anything is unclear, write to us.

## Who we are

UDVASITO (উদ্ভাসিত) is a free blood-donor platform run by **UDVASITO — a student initiative, University of Chittagong**. It connects people who need blood with willing donors, starting in Chattogram. A team member acts as our data-protection lead.

Contact: [udvasitobd@gmail.com](mailto:udvasitobd@gmail.com)

UDVASITO is not a blood bank, a hospital or a medical service. It does not sell anything, show advertising or sell your data.

## Our promise in short

- We collect only what is needed to find blood quickly and safely.
- Your phone number is never public. It is shown only to the people listed under "Who can see what", and every time someone sees it, we record it.
- We never store your live location or location history.
- Before any text goes to an AI service, our code removes phone numbers, names and illnesses. A person always checks what the AI suggests, and AI never decides who is contacted.
- You can ask for a copy of your data, correct it or delete it.

## Who can use UDVASITO

You must be **18 or older**. If you give a date of birth under 18, we stop the sign-up. We keep that date of birth and your Google sign-in (name and email address), so the same account cannot simply sign up again. We do not keep your phone number or anything else you typed.

## What we collect and why

### When you sign in

- **Your name and email address from Google** — to create your account and sign you in. We never post anything to your Google account.
- **Sign-in session details** (the network address and the type of device or browser you signed in from) — to keep you signed in and to stop mass fake sign-ups (only a few new profiles per network per hour).

### Your profile

- **Full name** — only your first name is shown to others.
- **Date of birth** — to confirm you are 18 or older and, for donors, within the donor age range.
- **Mobile number** — so the people you are matched with can call you, and so we can verify it is really yours.
- **Language** — to show the app in English or Bangla.
- **Consent records** (which version of this notice and the terms you accepted, and when) — the law requires us to keep proof of consent.
- **Account status and last activity** — to keep the service safe and to stop alerts to accounts nobody uses any more.
- **An invitation or campaign code**, if you joined through one — to know which blood drive brought you.

### Phone verification

- **Through Telegram:** your Telegram account ID and the number Telegram shares with our bot when you agree. We use them only to check that the number matches your profile.
- **In person:** which admin or volunteer verified you and when. The verifier checks only the last four digits; they never see your full number on their screen.

### If you become a donor (separate consent)

Donor details are health-related, so we ask for your agreement separately before you become a donor.

- **Blood group** — to match you with requests your blood can help.
- **Gender** — the minimum gap between donations is different for men and women.
- **Area** (division, district, upazila and an optional area name) and **how far you can travel** — to alert donors near the patient first.
- **Institution** (optional) — for campus blood drives.
- **Date and type of your last donation** — to alert you only when you are allowed to donate again.
- **Platelet donation preference, availability, pause dates and alert settings** (daily limit, quiet hours) — to respect your choices.
- **Your "I meet the health conditions" tick** (only the time you ticked it, not your answers) — we ask it each time you say "I can help".

### When you ask for blood

- **Blood group, whole blood or platelets, number of bags, hospital or area, the time it is needed by, and urgency** — the basic request that donors see.
- **Contact number** — your own number, shown to confirmed donors.
- **Optional details:** patient name, ward or bed and a note — shown only to confirmed donors. Ask the patient or their family before you enter these.
- **Optional hospital slip photo** — seen only by our volunteers and admins, to check the request by hand. It is deleted when the request closes.
- **Location:** if you tap "Use my current location", your phone or browser uses your position to find nearby hospitals. We store only the hospital you choose, or your area and an approximate point for that area, never your exact position.

### When you answer or take part

- **Your answers to requests** ("I can help" or "Can't this time", with an optional reason) and whether the donation happened — to confirm donors, keep standby donors ready and keep track records fair.
- **Who saw whose number, when and why** — every time a phone number is shown, it is recorded.
- **Alerts we sent and whether they were opened** — to send the next alert wave to the right number of donors.
- **Reports and blocks** you make or receive — to keep the community safe.
- **If you apply as a volunteer:** your areas, institution, motivation and whether you agree to show your number to requesters.

### From your device

- **Notification address** (a push token, or for web a push subscription with the browser type) — to deliver alerts.
- **Error reports** (app version, device model and system version, what went wrong) — to fix problems. Phone numbers are removed before a report leaves your device.
- **Simple usage events inside UDVASITO**, such as "an alert was opened". They never contain phone numbers. We use no advertising and no third-party analytics.

## Why we may use your data (legal basis)

We process your data on the basis of **your consent**, which you give on the first screen. Donor and health-related details are covered by a separate donor consent. You can withdraw consent at any time (see "Your rights"). Bangladesh's Personal Data Protection Ordinance 2025 treats health data as sensitive, and we handle it that way.

## Who can see what

- **Anyone with a request link:** the blood group, whole blood or platelets, number of bags, hospital, area, needed-by time and the verified badge. Never names or phone numbers.
- **Donors who receive an alert:** the same basics and the requester's track record (member since, number of requests, confirmed donations).
- **Confirmed donors:** also the patient name, ward or bed and the requester's phone number.
- **The requester:** the first name, blood group and area of each confirmed donor, and that donor's phone number, unless the donor chose "I'll call them". Also the first name and phone number of up to two volunteers helping while the request is open.
- **Volunteers:** the request details — including the patient name, ward or bed, the requester's phone number and the slip photo — and donor search results (first name, blood group, area). They see a donor's phone number only if that donor agreed to be called by volunteers, and they must give a reason each time.
- **Admins:** what they need to keep the service safe. A donor's phone number stays hidden until an admin chooses to reveal it and gives a reason, which is recorded.
- **Nobody:** your exact location (it is never stored), and the audit log and consent records (admins only).

Numbers are shown only for a limited time around the request.

## AI and voice

- **AI:** when you type or paste a request, the text may be sent to Google's Gemini AI service to fill in the form. Our code first removes phone numbers, names, illnesses, email addresses and ID numbers. Every suggestion is marked "AI suggested" and you check it before posting. Because we use Google's free service, Google may use this cleaned text to improve its services. We keep the cleaned text and the AI's answer for **30 days** to check quality. The app works without AI.
- **Voice:** your phone's or browser's own speech service turns your voice into text (this may be Google's service). UDVASITO never receives or stores your audio, and audio never goes to the AI service.

## Services that process data for us

We use these providers to run UDVASITO. Each gets only what its job needs:

- **Convex** — our database and server (in the European Union).
- **Cloudflare** — hosts the website and protects it from attacks.
- **Google** — sign-in, the Gemini AI service and Firebase Cloud Messaging (delivers Android alerts).
- **Expo** — delivers alerts to the app and app updates.
- **Your browser's push service** — delivers website alerts.
- **Telegram** — phone verification, only if you choose it.
- **Cloudinary** — creates the pictures for sharing a request (request basics only, no names or phone numbers).
- **Sentry** — error reports, with phone numbers removed.

## Data stored outside Bangladesh

These providers store or process data on servers outside Bangladesh, mainly in the European Union and the United States. We choose providers with strong security, send them as little as possible and never send phone numbers to error reports, alerts text or the AI service.

## How long we keep data

- **Account and donor details:** until you delete your account. Deletion is completed within **30 days**.
- **Requests:** patient name, ward and phone numbers are removed **30 days after the request closes**. The rest is kept without personal details for statistics.
- **Hospital slip photos:** deleted when the request closes, at the latest **7 days** after.
- **Records of who saw a number, the audit log and consent records:** **12 months**.
- **AI input (already cleaned) and output:** **30 days**.
- **Notification addresses:** until you sign out, uninstall the app, or after **60 days** without use.

## Your rights

You can:

- **get a copy** of your data;
- **correct** wrong data;
- **delete** your account and data;
- **withdraw your consent** — as a donor you can also pause or hide your donor profile at any time.

Write to [udvasitobd@gmail.com](mailto:udvasitobd@gmail.com) from the email address you sign in with, so we know it is you. Records that the law requires us to keep (for example consent records) are kept for the time listed above and then deleted. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you may also complain to the authority responsible for data protection in Bangladesh.

## Security

Data travels encrypted. Access to personal data is limited to what each role needs, and every phone-number reveal and every admin action is recorded. The practice area is kept fully separate from real requests. If a data breach puts you at risk, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

## Changes to this notice

When this notice changes, the version and date at the top change, and the app asks you to read and accept the new version before you continue.
